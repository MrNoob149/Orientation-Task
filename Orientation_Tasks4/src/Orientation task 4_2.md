### QUESTION
Consider the categories in which performance measures are maintained/updated/computed.
(Think in general terms: service point/customer; not in target system terms.)

### ANSWER

In this question, we will have the following:
- Customer (which is entity)
- The service point
- The bank (which is the server)
 
1) When the customer arrive, they will have to wait for their turn (queue), and then will start working with the
bank (service time), and finally leave when they are done.
The time when the customer arrive, waiting and leave will be updated.

2)  When the customer arrive, the service point will work with them (time working with entity)
and then the number of clients that the service point worked with will be recorded down (number of entity served)

3)  The bank (system) will calculate the following:
- The total time from the moment the customer arrive and leave.
- The average time the customer work in the service point
- The total number of clients who arrive at the bank in the stimulation
